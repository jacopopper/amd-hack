01 Capture · 03 3D reconstruction

From flat photos to a room.

VGGT is a transformer that looks at all the photos at once. In a single pass it works out where each one was taken from, how far away every pixel is, and where it sits in space.

  1. Camera poses: where each photo was taken
  2. Depth: the distance of every pixel
  3. 3D points: the room, with each hazard in its place
VGGT-1B · Meta, CVPR 2025Gaussian splatting · gsplatPyTorch · ROCm
↓ 39 phone photos
real prototype output

Real output: 39 phone photos of a real home, camera poses from VGGT-1B, 757,792 Gaussians trained with gsplat. The photographs remain the evidence; the 3D view helps the reviewer navigate. Scale not yet calibrated.

12

Who it is for

Built around the discharge, not around the AI.

Uses it

The discharge nurse

Reviews a home in minutes, from the ward, before the patient leaves. Decides what goes to the family.

Benefits

The patient and the family

A clear plan before coming home. A relative takes the photos; the same relative receives the actions.

Adopts it

Hospitals and local health services

Their discharge pathway gains the home review the CDC recommends, on hardware they control.

Fills a real gap

With one occupational therapist for every ~5,000 people over 65, the real alternative to a home visit is no assessment at all.

Uses the scarce expert well

“Check in person” sends to a home visit only what the photos cannot settle.

Fits clinical rules

A professional signs off every finding; data stays inside the hospital.

Next

  • Self-hosted pipeline on one AMD Instinct MI300X with ROCm
